Modified for Roadster: public/models/car.glb is built from it by scripts/build-car-model.ts.
The build decimates the mesh (500k to about 140k triangles), rebuilds all four wheels from
one rear wheel (the fused front wheels are cut out of the body and dark arch liners added),
splits the single material into paint, glass, carbon trim, tail lights, tyres and rims (the
paint drawn from a recolourable grey version of the texture), adds a front light bar,
rescales and re-centres the car, and compresses the geometry (Meshopt) and textures (WebP).
